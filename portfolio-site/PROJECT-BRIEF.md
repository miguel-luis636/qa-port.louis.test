# Cyberpunk QA Portfolio — Project Brief

## Objective
Build a professional QA Engineer portfolio for Miguel Luis, using the supplied cyberpunk portfolio screenshots as the primary visual reference.

## Visual direction
- Dark near-black background with subtle grid/scanline texture.
- Cyan, violet and magenta neon accents.
- Futuristic display typography for headings; readable monospace typography for terminal labels and metadata.
- Angular technical panels, thin neon borders, restrained glow and glitch effects.
- Responsive layout; preserve readability and accessibility.
- Do not imitate a generic CV template. The site should feel like a futuristic QA operations environment.

## Entry experience
A short, skippable boot sequence before the portfolio:
1. INITIALIZING QA ENVIRONMENT
2. IDENTITY: MIGUEL LUIS
3. ROLE: QA ENGINEER
4. SYSTEM STATUS: ONLINE
Then transition to the home section. Keep it brief, respect reduced-motion preferences, and do not replay on every in-page navigation.

## Information architecture
00. INTRO — boot sequence.
01. HOME — professional headline, role, specialties and primary calls to action.
02. ABOUT — professional summary, QA mindset, certifications and development goals.
03. EXPERIENCE — paid employment, freelance work and volunteer experience, clearly distinguished.
04. MISSIONS — selected technical projects and case studies.
05. QA LAB — skills and tools grouped by testing discipline.
06. RECORDS — certifications, recognition and relevant achievements.
07. CONTACT — GitHub, LinkedIn and professional contact.
